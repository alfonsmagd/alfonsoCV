01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I build the systems
behind the pixels.

I'm Alfonso Mateos Aparicio, a Render Programmer at Ubisoft–Snowdrop, working in World Domain since January 2026. I bring over 10 years of professional experience in globally recognised technology sectors, with a C/C++ engineering background spanning computer graphics, game development and real-time simulation.

I created IFNITY, a hardware-agnostic rendering interface for OpenGL, Vulkan and Direct3D 12, and LightDX12 (LDX12), a C++20 graphics library for rapidly building and prototyping with modern DirectX 12 features.

LDX12 brings together bindless resource access, multidraw, raster and compute. I'm currently working to integrate ray tracing into its architecture and migrating Flycast, a Dreamcast emulator, to LDX12.

I've ported games such as GTA III to DirectX 12, and I actively contribute to open-source graphics projects, including Khronos/Slang and NVIDIA Donut.

My experience also includes Unreal Engine 4/5, C++ game mechanics and physics, code optimisation through metaprogramming, and GPU programming to improve rendering algorithms and reduce computational costs.

Away from the screen: animals, the outdoors, and time between Spain and Mexico.

July 2024 — July 2025

A hardware-agnostic rendering interface with an RHI architecture based on NVIDIA Rendering Hardware Interface. One architecture, three graphics APIs.

Bindless rendering, MultiDrawIndirect and cross-API performance benchmarks. Awarded the highest grade for my master's thesis at URJC.

Technical details +
  • Vulkan 1.3 implementation with modern techniques
  • DirectX 12 with Shader Model 6.6
  • OpenGL 4.6 compatibility layer
  • MultiDrawIndirect for efficient batch rendering
  • Bindless Rendering for optimal performance
  • Benchmarking system for scene performance comparison
  • NVIDIA RHI-based architecture
